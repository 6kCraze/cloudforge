01 / INSIDE THE SYSTEM

A small city.
A durable promise.

One upload sets a real distributed system in motion. Each building has a job. PostgreSQL keeps the record.

  1. 01—02

    Accept. Persist.

    The API saves your images and records each job with its delivery intent in one database transaction.

  2. 03—05

    Dispatch. Execute.

    The dispatcher publishes to RabbitMQ. A Go worker claims the job, renews its lease and makes a thumbnail.

  3. ↻ / 06

    Recover. Deliver.

    Expired work gets a bounded retry through the outbox. Only the winning attempt can publish the result you download.
